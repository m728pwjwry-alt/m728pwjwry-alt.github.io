About — Hot Springs, Arkansas

A decade in the dirt, and in the business of it.

I'm Justin Baird. I've been digging, buying, cleaning, and selling minerals for about ten years, and it's been my full-time work since 2017. I work out of Hot Springs, Arkansas — right in the middle of the best crystal country in the world.

Most of what I sell is either dug by hand here in the Ouachitas or bought direct from the miners who dug it, here and overseas. I'd rather tell you honestly what something is than talk it up into what it isn't.

When I'm not on a dig I'm at the cleaning bench. Everything that leaves here gets the clay and iron worked off it by hand and gets prepped and trimmed before it's photographed — slow work, but it's the difference between a rock and a specimen.
